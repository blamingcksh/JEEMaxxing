WHAT IT IS
----------
JEEMaxxing is a client-side JEE-prep grind command center: a single offline
PWA that turns studying into a closed feedback loop. You lock in with focus
timers, feed it questions (manually or via AI), practice against an Elo-style
skill rating that adapts to you, review mistakes with spaced repetition, and
watch a virtual island grow with your effort. Accountability systems actively
police procrastination - scheduled checkpoints, late-night rating decay, and
daily boss-fight bounties - while recovery systems (fatigue meter, deload
days, lifeline) protect you from burnout. Everything runs in the browser:
no server, no build step, all data in IndexedDB with optional Google Drive
backup.

In one line: a self-coaching exam grinder that measures your skill like a
competitive game, schedules your memory like Anki, and pressures you like a
drill sergeant - while a little world grows in the background.


ARCHITECTURE AT A GLANCE
------------------------
- Vanilla ES6 modules, zero framework/bundler. Serve over HTTP; installable
  PWA (service worker: network-first HTML, cached assets, fully offline).
- storage.js owns AppState = single source of truth; IndexedDB (jeemaxxing_db)
  persists everything; localStorage only caches small prefs.
- app.js (~11k lines) is the orchestrator: boot/day-cycle, question bank,
  ingestion, practice, Elo math, streaks, dashboard wiring.
- Feature modules plug in without circular imports via window.* bridges:
  pomodoro (timers), matrix (vault/SR), checkpoint (accountability),
  cns-load/deload/lifeline/nightguard (recovery), gallery-break/metronome/
  focus-sound/fx (rituals + feedback), grove-islands/forest-bg/candlestick-
  engine (visuals), theme/dashboard-clean/boot-sequence (shell).
- Multi-tab safe: BroadcastChannel merges counters across open tabs.


THE CORE LOOP
-------------
 1. LOCK IN - Start a sprint (Pomodoro or stopwatch). Solves earn a real x1.5
    ELO bonus while the timer runs; soundscapes auto-play; quitting mid-block
    breaks your chain and logs a forfeit in the focus ledger.
 2. FEED - Add questions four ways: screenshot multi-crop + OCR, Gemini Gem
    JSON dumps (auto difficulty stamping, anti-cheat audits, LaTeX healing,
    diagram auto-crop from tagged screenshots), answer-key mapping, or manual
    entry. Duplicates are detected and purged automatically.
 3. GRIND - Practice in Standard, Flow (moderately hard) or Hardcore (brutal,
    high-reward) mode. Every solve feeds the Elo engine.
 4. RATE - Elo/MMR per subject plus a global rating: your time vs expected,
    chapter health, fatigue and mode all move the numbers. Ranks climb from
    NPC to Unhinged Gigachad; an AIR popup projects your All-India Rank.
 5. HEAL - Wrong answers go to the Error Vault; SuperMemo-2-style spaced
    repetition resurfaces them weakest-memory-first until mastered.
 6. GROW - Solves water a persistent island world; daily target rings close
    the loop on the sidebar; missed days put your streak and trees at risk.


MAJOR SYSTEMS (conceptually)
----------------------------
FOCUS ENGINE (pomodoro.js + focus-sound.js)
  Countdown/stopwatch/rounds with wall-clock accuracy; dynamic subject
  switching; an always-visible Sprint Widget; a per-day ledger tracking
  blocks completed, current chain and forfeits; session receipts showing
  exact ELO earned per block; procedural soundscapes (rain/cafe/fire/noise)
  that duck under the end-of-session bell.

ELO / MMR ENGINE (app.js, tunings in storage.js)
  A chess-like rating per subject. Questions carry their own difficulty
  (qElo): AI-stamped ones are trusted after validation, others are learned
  from your results. Wins and losses move both your rating and the question's
  estimated difficulty, weighted by solve speed, chapter health, fatigue,
  late-night penalties and active mode. First-attempt accuracy only - retries
  never inflate stats. Global rating punishes unbalanced subjects.

ERROR VAULT + SPACED REPETITION (matrix.js)
  Every mistake is vaulted with a friction tag (concept / calc / misread).
  Items are scheduled by a tuned SM-2 algorithm where honesty matters:
  rating your autonomy and error type changes interval growth (a perfect
  solve grows roughly 8x faster than a blind-approach guess). Chapter health
  is the decayed memory strength of all its items - it drives the Retention
  grid, nav warnings, and checkpoint question selection. A Daily Fix Queue
  auto-picks today's weakest memories within per-subject caps.

ACCOUNTABILITY (checkpoint.js + nightguard.js + bounty)
  Scheduled checkpoints arm when you idle too long; miss the grace window
  and a lockdown forces a Proof-of-Work solve - failure scars Protocol Zero
  onto your graphs. After 23:00 a Night Guard progressively decays ELO gains
  (x0.80 -> x0.55 -> x0.20 force-set) unless you hold-to-override. A daily
  Bounty Boss dares you to clutch a previously-missed question in 180 s:
  win = tripled supercharged payoffs; loss = 24h lockout plus a target tax.

RECOVERY (cns-load.js + lifeline.js + deload.js + mood calibration)
  A CNS fatigue meter rises with study volume; high load gates hardcore
  modes and activates a Lifeline that eases difficulty at 35% reduced yield.
  Sustained strain can schedule Deload rest days that preserve your streak.
  Mood calibration scales daily targets; accumulated sleep debt caps harder.

GAMIFICATION & WORLD (grove-islands.js + fx.js + CK rail)
  Cumulative study seconds grow trees on an island archipelago - harder
  solves plant rarer, faster-growing flora. Streak flames pixel-animate
  hotter with combo; wrong answers may consume a shield. Sidebar rings track
  physics/chemistry/maths/fix targets; closing all four shows LOOP CLOSED.
  Emoji bursts, ripples, haptics and synth SFX everywhere - all toggleable
  for photosensitivity or reduced-motion users.

DASHBOARD & ANALYTICS (index.html bento + candlestick-engine.js)
  Draggable bento cards: Today's Progress triple ring, Momentum candlesticks
  of a custom cognitive-yield index (with trend detection and 5-day
  prediction), a GitHub-style year heatmap of daily variance, Chapter
  Progress and Retention Health. Layout, density and card sizes persist.

CLOUD & DATA (storage.js)
  Optional Google Drive folder mirrors state plus question images. Merge is
  conflict-tolerant: high-water daily counters, last-write-wins ratings, and
  tombstones so deletes never resurrect. Auto-push every 30s throttle +
  2-minute heartbeat + manual Sync Now. Persistence failures surface a
  warning banner - never silent data loss.

UI / THEMES (styles.css + theme.js)
  Token-based design system: 7 accent themes (Furnace, Synthwave, Glacier,
  Overgrowth, Blood Moon, Sakura, Stealth) x Midnight/Dusk appearance modes,
  all re-skinning live via CSS variables. Heavy glassmorphism, 53 keyframe
  animations, full reduced-motion support, responsive down to 480px.


DATA MODEL IN ONE BREATH
------------------------
questionBank[] holds every question: text, options, answer, type (MCQ single
or multi, numeric, or free-response), optional diagram/option/solution
images (local or fetched from Drive), qElo difficulty plus provenance
(gem-stamped vs learned), SM-2 fields (interval, ease factor, due date,
capped attempt history), status lifecycle (unsolved -> solved/wrong/error),
friction reason, skip ledger, and anti-cheat stamps. Around it: per-subject
daily solved counts and study seconds, a permanent day-by-day history ledger
(feeds streaks, graphs and the world), elo {physics, chemistry, maths,
global} with an update clock, daily targets with a 24h edit lock, bounty
state, mood multiplier, practice mode with hardcore daily cap, Drive tokens
and tombstones. Images live in a persistent vault inside IndexedDB.


KEY NUMBERS WORTH KNOWING
-------------------------
  Elo starts at 1200 (soft cap ~3000); K = 12 physics/chem, 16 maths
  Flow P_win window 0.75-0.85; Hardcore 0.35-0.50, capped 8 solves/day
  Lifeline activates at CNS >= 0.80 (yield x0.65), disables below 0.40
  Night Guard tiers x0.80 / x0.55 / x0.20 from 23:00 (force-set 03:00)
  SM-2 mastery = interval > 30 days && ease > 2.5; ease floor 1.3
  Fix Queue caps: 5 physics, 5 maths, 10 chemistry - weakest memory first
  Deep-work bonus x1.5 while a timer runs; bounty clock is 180 seconds
  Default targets 10/10/10 solves + 5/5/5 fixes, locked 24h once saved
  AIR projection: Adv 10^(8.00 - 0.00214*E); Main 10^(8.61 - 0.00236*E)


RUNNING & TOOLING
-----------------
  python3 -m http.server 8000   (any static server) -> http://localhost:8000
  No build step. node_modules exists only for Playwright QA harnesses in
  scripts/ (npm run smoke / qa / qa-focus / qa-progress). KaTeX and three.js
  are vendored locally; icons and ambient WAVs come from generator scripts.


KNOWN QUIRKS (code reality vs README)
-------------------------------------
- Checkpoint monitoring loop is currently deactivated in source (system
  armed but passive; old penalties remain as graph scars).
- nightguard.js is the ELO-decay sleep guard, not a maintenance job.
- The old 15-day vault sparkline was removed (rail counters remain).
- Time Bank card is not injected by dashboard-clean.js despite README claims.
